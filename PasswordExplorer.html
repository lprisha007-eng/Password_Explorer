💡 Password Improvement Tips

Follow these recommendations to improve password security.

📏

Prioritize Length

Use long passwords or passphrases. Aim for at least 15 characters.

🎲

Be Unpredictable

Avoid names, birthdays, dictionary words and common patterns.

🔀

Use Variety

Include a variety of character types where appropriate.

🔒

Unique Passwords

Use a different password for every important account.

🗝️

Use a Password Manager

Use a reputable password manager to generate and store unique passwords.

📱

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Add an extra layer of protection beyond your password.
